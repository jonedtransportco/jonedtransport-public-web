# 02A.15A Contractor Intake Route Matrix v1.0

Date: 2026-07-28  
Depends on: `02A.15_Contractor_Intake_Requirements_Normalization_v1.0.md`  
Purpose: define a cleaner applicant routing model for the future public web flow using the real intake dimensions that matter operationally:

- applicant form: `person` or `business`
- vehicle path: `with vehicle` or `without vehicle`
- license path: `CDL` or `Non-CDL`

This replaces the earlier over-simplified route model based only on labels like `owner operator` or `contractor`.

## Scope

This matrix is a planning artifact only.

It does not:

- open public intake;
- connect real systems;
- activate portal workflows;
- approve operational onboarding automation.

It does:

- define route logic for the future public site;
- clarify which requirements are universal vs conditional;
- reduce friction for applicants who may apply as a person or as a business;
- avoid treating `Owner Operator` and `Contractor` as the only top-level categories.

## Core design rule

The public site should not begin by forcing the applicant to choose a legal or business label they may not interpret the same way JONED does.

The public site should first ask:

1. Are you applying as a `person` or as a `business`?
2. Are you applying `with a vehicle` or `without a vehicle`?
3. Does your path require `CDL` or `Non-CDL`?

Only after those answers should the site generate the checklist and later classify the route internally for JONED.

## Primary routing dimensions

### Dimension A: applicant form

- `Person`
- `Business`
- `Not sure yet`

The public intake should allow `Not sure yet` so the form does not create an avoidable barrier.

### Dimension B: vehicle path

- `With vehicle`
- `Without vehicle`
- `Not sure yet`

Operational meaning:

- `With vehicle` usually maps to owner-operator or contractor-like paths where the applicant brings a unit.
- `Without vehicle` usually maps to driver-like or worker-like paths where the applicant does not bring a unit.

### Dimension C: license path

- `CDL`
- `Non-CDL`
- `Not sure yet`

The site should never assume CDL is universal.

## Operational route framework

This is the recommended internal route model after those answers are captured.

### Route P1

`Person · With vehicle · CDL`

Typical use:

- individual applicant;
- enters with own or controlled vehicle;
- CDL path;
- likely closest to traditional owner-operator intake.

### Route P2

`Person · With vehicle · Non-CDL`

Typical use:

- individual applicant;
- enters with vehicle;
- non-CDL path;
- should remain available because not all contractor profiles require CDL.

### Route P3

`Person · Without vehicle · CDL`

Typical use:

- driver candidate;
- person-first path;
- no vehicle brought into intake;
- CDL path.

### Route P4

`Person · Without vehicle · Non-CDL`

Typical use:

- driver or worker candidate;
- person-first path;
- no vehicle at intake;
- non-CDL path.

### Route B1

`Business · With vehicle · CDL`

Typical use:

- business applicant;
- vehicle-based path;
- CDL-linked operation;
- likely commercial authority and insurance-heavy.

### Route B2

`Business · With vehicle · Non-CDL`

Typical use:

- business applicant;
- vehicle-based path;
- non-CDL path;
- still may require registration, insurance, and some authority review depending on operation.

### Route B3

`Business · Without vehicle · CDL`

Typical use:

- less common;
- keep possible in the model, but validate whether this truly exists in JONED's intake reality before public release.

### Route B4

`Business · Without vehicle · Non-CDL`

Typical use:

- less common;
- keep structurally possible, but treat as lower-priority until operationally confirmed.

## Recommended public-first routes

For the first public implementation, the most practical routes are likely:

- `P1 Person · With vehicle · CDL`
- `P2 Person · With vehicle · Non-CDL`
- `P3 Person · Without vehicle · CDL`
- `P4 Person · Without vehicle · Non-CDL`
- `B1 Business · With vehicle · CDL`
- `B2 Business · With vehicle · Non-CDL`

Routes `B3` and `B4` should remain modeled, but may not need to be public on day one unless JONED confirms those are real intake scenarios.

## Universal intake fields

These fields should appear in all routes:

- applicant form
- legal name
- business name if applicable
- phone number
- email
- city
- state
- preferred language
- vehicle path
- license path

## Interpretation rules

### Person vs business

This is not the same as:

- `owner operator`
- `contractor`

Those labels may still be used later internally, but the public web should not rely on them as the first branching mechanism.

### With vehicle vs without vehicle

This is one of the most important routing decisions.

Operationally:

- `With vehicle` usually means the applicant brings a unit into the relationship.
- `Without vehicle` usually means the applicant is entering as a driver or worker path under JONED-managed vehicle operations.

### CDL vs Non-CDL

This should always remain an explicit branch.  
The site should never imply:

- everyone needs CDL;
- everyone without vehicle is non-CDL;
- everyone with vehicle is CDL.

## Vehicle overlay

Once the applicant is in a `with vehicle` route, the site should apply this second-level logic:

| Vehicle type | Registration | COI | Auto liability | Cargo | Trailer interchange | ELD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Passenger / light vehicle | Conditional | Conditional | Conditional | No by default | No | No by default |
| Box truck | Yes | Yes | Yes | Conditional | No by default | Conditional |
| Straight truck | Yes | Yes | Yes | Conditional | No by default | Conditional |
| Tractor | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Conditional | Conditional |
| Trailer-attached operation | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Conditional-to-likely | Conditional |

## License overlay

| License path | License upload | Medical card | CDL-specific fields | Non-CDL-specific fields |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CDL | Yes | Conditional-to-likely | Yes | No |
| Non-CDL | Yes | No by default | No | Yes |

## Practical public-site mapping

### `jonedtransport.com/owner-operators`

This page should not force only one strict legal definition.  
It should serve applicants who are:

- people with vehicles;
- businesses with vehicles;
- CDL and non-CDL vehicle-based profiles.

### `jonedtransport.com/drivers`

This page should focus mainly on:

- people without vehicles;
- CDL and non-CDL driver paths.

## Recommended release order

1. public corporate site
2. `Owner Operators` public page using:
   - `P1`
   - `P2`
   - `B1`
   - `B2`
3. `Drivers` public page using:
   - `P3`
   - `P4`
4. optional expansion for `B3` and `B4` if they are operationally real

## Open policy items

These still need JONED operational clarification later:

- whether a person with vehicle but without formal business should always provide W-9 at intake;
- whether business-without-vehicle routes are truly needed in public launch;
- when voided check belongs in intake vs later review;
- exactly which vehicle classes require ELD in approved policy;
- whether agreements differ between person-based and business-based applicants.

## Result

This route matrix is cleaner because it reflects what the applicant actually knows about themselves:

- person or business;
- with vehicle or without vehicle;
- CDL or non-CDL.

That creates a more functional public experience and reduces the chance of building the wrong intake barriers into the website.
