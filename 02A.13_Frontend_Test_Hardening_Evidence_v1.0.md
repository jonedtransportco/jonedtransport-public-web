- `Solicitar reporte`, `Abrir dashboard`, and `Exportar vista` remain tied to their approved permissions and state conditions;
- internal tabs continue to include `Catálogo`, `Widgets`, and `Programación`;
- `Programación` remains explicitly non-operational;
- `Reporting API` remains future-only through the adapter boundary.

### api-real rollback protections

- frontend keeps the `Reportes` mixed-mode gate behind explicit integration flags;
- fallback to mock for `Reportes` remains asserted;
- non-operational actions remain filtered when `api-real` is active;
- the controlled route still enforces:
  - `REPORTS_API_MODE_DISABLED`
  - `REPORTS_API_URL_MISSING`
  - `REPORTS_UPSTREAM_UNREACHABLE`
  - `no-store` behavior
