## Implemented state

### 1. Public `Drivers` page

`app/drivers/page.tsx` now provides a dedicated recruiting-facing public route for driver applicants.

The page is intentionally framed around:

- people without a self-provided vehicle route;
- CDL and Non-CDL driver paths;
- public intake clarity before internal review;
- no mixing with owner-operator intake or portal-only workflow handling.

### 2. `Owner Operators` step-based mock prequalification

`app/owner-operators/prequalification-form.tsx` now provides a non-operational step-based public flow that covers:

1. route questions;
2. applicant information;
3. license and compliance;
4. conditional route-specific requirements;
5. review and mock submit.

The flow resolves the approved internal route model:

- `P1`
- `P2`
- `P3`
- `P4`
- `B1`
- `B2`
- `B3`
- `B4`

It also computes a route-aware upload checklist and conditionally shows:

- vehicle block;
- authority block;
- insurance block;
- tax/payment block.

### 3. Public recruiting structure

`app/page.tsx` now exposes both public recruiting paths explicitly:

- `Owner Operators`
- `Drivers`

This removes the earlier placeholder framing for `Drivers` and makes the external recruiting structure materially more complete.
