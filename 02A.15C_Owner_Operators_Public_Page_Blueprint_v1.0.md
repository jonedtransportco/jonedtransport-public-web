# 02A.15C Owner Operators Public Page Blueprint v1.0

Date: 2026-07-28  
Depends on:

- `02A.15_Contractor_Intake_Requirements_Normalization_v1.0.md`
- `02A.15A_Contractor_Intake_Route_Matrix_v1.0.md`
- `02A.15B_Contractor_Intake_Form_and_Upload_Blueprint_v1.0.md`

Purpose: define the public-facing `Owner Operators` page for `jonedtransport.com` so the site can:

- explain the opportunity clearly;
- welcome multiple applicant shapes without creating early barriers;
- connect directly into the step-based intake flow;
- stay consistent with the future separation between public website and internal portal.

## Scope

This blueprint is public-site planning only.

It does not:

- activate public application intake;
- approve applicant requirements;
- connect uploads or storage;
- create operational onboarding.

It does:

- define page sections;
- define messaging hierarchy;
- define CTA placement;
- define how the page hands off into the intake flow.

## Page objective

The page should answer four questions fast:

1. Is this page for me?
2. What kind of profiles can apply?
3. What should I have ready before I start?
4. How do I begin?

## URL

Recommended public URL:

`jonedtransport.com/owner-operators`

## Position in website navigation

This page should be linked from:

- main navigation
- homepage hero
- homepage recruiting or partner block
- footer

Recommended menu label:

- `Owner Operators`

## High-level page structure

1. Hero
2. Fit and profile clarification
3. Why work with JONED
4. Who can apply
5. What to prepare
6. How the process works
7. Start application CTA block
8. FAQ
9. Footer CTA

## 1. Hero

### Goal

Make the page feel welcoming, serious, and easy to start.

### Content

#### Eyebrow

`Owner Operators`

#### Headline

`Apply to work with JONED in the path that fits your operation.`

#### Supporting text

`Whether you are applying as a person or as a business, with a vehicle or without one, we will guide you through the requirements that apply to your profile.`

#### Primary CTA

`Start application`

#### Secondary CTA

`See what to prepare`

### Messaging rule

The hero should not over-commit to one narrow applicant type.

Avoid:

- language that assumes everyone has a CDL;
- language that assumes everyone is a formal company;
- language that assumes everyone brings a tractor.

## 2. Fit and profile clarification

### Goal

Reduce confusion about whether the page is only for one traditional owner-operator pattern.

### Section title

`Who this path is for`

### Body copy

`This application path is designed for applicants who may work with JONED in different operating setups. Some apply as individuals, some as businesses, some bring a vehicle, and some apply for a role that does not begin with a vehicle at intake.`

### Clarifying bullets

- `Applying as a person or as a business`
- `Applying with a vehicle or without a vehicle`
- `CDL and Non-CDL paths`
- `Requirements shown according to your profile`

## 3. Why work with JONED

### Goal

Make the opportunity attractive without turning the page into vague recruiting copy.

### Section title

`Why work with JONED`

### Suggested card blocks

- `Structured process`
  - `A clearer intake and review path from the beginning.`

- `Professional communication`
  - `A more organized experience for applicants and operating partners.`

- `Profile-based requirements`
  - `You will see the requirements that match your route instead of one universal checklist.`

- `Operational continuity`
  - `Designed to support long-term working relationships, not only one-time submission.`

## 4. Who can apply

### Goal

Give applicants enough clarity before the form begins.

### Section title

`Who can apply through this page`

### Structure

Present this as route-friendly, not legalistically rigid.

#### Group A

`Person with vehicle`

Examples:

- individuals entering with a vehicle;
- CDL or Non-CDL depending on the operation.

#### Group B

`Business with vehicle`

Examples:

- business applicants entering with operational vehicle support;
- may include authority and insurance review depending on the route.

#### Group C

`Person without vehicle`

Examples:

- applicants entering on a driver-style path;
- CDL or Non-CDL depending on the role.

### Important note

`If you are not sure which category fits you, you can still start the application. The form will guide you.`

## 5. What to prepare

### Goal

Give useful preparation guidance without dumping the entire conditional logic.

### Section title

`What to prepare before you start`

### Intro text

`You do not need every document listed below unless it applies to your route. We will show the exact requirements after a few quick questions.`

### Preparation groups

#### Group 1: applicant information

- legal name
- business name if applicable
- phone number
- email
- city and state

#### Group 2: license and compliance

- license information
- CDL or Non-CDL details
- medical card only if applicable

#### Group 3: vehicle information

- vehicle type
- registration
- unit details if applicable

#### Group 4: business and authority

- DOT
- MC
- commercial authority documents

Only if applicable.

#### Group 5: insurance and financial setup

- insurance certificate
- applicable coverage information
- W-9
- voided check

Only if applicable.

## 6. How the process works

### Goal

Make the intake feel understandable and finite.

### Section title

`How the process works`

### Suggested 4-step flow

1. `Tell us about your path`
   - `Answer a few questions about how you are applying.`

2. `See your requirements`
   - `We show the fields and documents that match your route.`

3. `Submit your information`
   - `Complete the form and upload the documents you have available.`

4. `JONED reviews your application`
   - `Our team reviews your submission and follows up if more information is needed.`

### Messaging rule

Do not claim:

- instant approval;
- operational activation;
- real-time onboarding.

## 7. Start application CTA block

### Goal

Create a strong conversion point after expectations have been set.

### Section title

`Ready to begin?`

### Body copy

`Start your application and we will guide you through the requirements that match your profile.`

### CTA

`Start application`

### Secondary note

`If you are not sure whether your path is CDL, Non-CDL, with vehicle, or without vehicle, you can still continue.`

## 8. FAQ

### Goal

Reduce hesitation before intake begins.

### Suggested FAQ items

#### `Do I need a CDL to apply?`

`Not always. Some routes require CDL and others follow a Non-CDL path. The application will help identify the right route.`

#### `Do I need to have a vehicle to start the process?`

`Not always. Some applicants enter with a vehicle and others apply through a no-vehicle path.`

#### `Do I need to apply as a company?`

`No. Some applicants apply as individuals and others apply as businesses.`

#### `Do I need every document before I begin?`

`No. The application will show the requirements that apply to your route, and some items may be requested later if needed.`

#### `Will I see the same checklist as everyone else?`

`No. The checklist is intended to adapt to your profile, license path, and vehicle path.`

## 9. Footer CTA

### Goal

Keep a final action visible at the bottom of the page.

### Copy

`Still interested in working with JONED? Start your application and we will guide you to the right path.`

### CTA

`Start application`

## UX and tone rules

### Tone

The page should feel:

- clear
- welcoming
- professional
- practical

### Avoid

- legal-heavy language at the top
- universal checklists
- hard assumptions about CDL
- hard assumptions about vehicle ownership
- hard assumptions about company formation

### Prefer

- route-based clarity
- simple language
- confidence without overpromising

## Handoff into intake flow

The page should connect to the dynamic intake described in `02A.15B`.

Recommended handoff:

- hero CTA -> intake start
- CTA block -> intake start
- footer CTA -> intake start

The page itself should not try to show the full dynamic form inline unless that becomes an explicit product decision later.

## Recommended first release behavior

For the first implementation:

- keep the page public;
- keep the intake route logic controlled;
- do not expose internal status handling;
- do not mix this page with internal portal navigation.

## Result

This page blueprint gives JONED a public-facing `Owner Operators` experience that:

- matches the route matrix;
- reduces applicant confusion;
- avoids premature barriers;
- prepares a clean bridge into the future application flow.
